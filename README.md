# tiny-pastures-modding-helper

A guide on how to create mods for the [Tiny Pasture steam game](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3167550/Tiny_Pasture/) (A cute animal idle desktop game).

1. You need to have bought and downloaded the Tiny Pasture game, as well as downloaded the Steam app in order to mod this game.
2. Look at the Tiny pasture mod guide: https://bcnege18gifk.feishu.cn/docx/MVh1dKMtToLAI2x2iOqcu2M5nCb

**Follow the steps below to get started:**

## [Setup](docs/setup.md)
How to decompile Tiny Pasture and get the Godot game engine setup ready to create your first mod

## [Creating animal assets](docs/assets.md)
How to create the assets and spritesheets for your new mod

## [Create and configure your mod](docs/create-mod.md)
Create the new mod in Godot ready to be tested and submitted

## [Export your mod](docs/export-mod.md)
Export your mod from Godot, test it locally and then upload it to the Steam workshop
